The Navodaya Routine

We hated the bell, but we miss the rhythm. A typical day at JNV Jalna.

05:30 AM

Morning PT Fall-in

Waking up in the freezing Marathwada winter, running to the ground half-asleep hoping the MOD doesn't catch you late.

08:00 AM

Morning Assembly

House lines, uniform checks, the news reading, and singing the Navodaya Anthem together before rushing to class.

01:30 PM

Mess Time (Lunch)

The stampede to the dining hall. Fighting for the best table and begging the seniors or mess bhaiya for an extra serving of dessert.

04:30 PM

Games & Sports

The best time of the day. Inter-house rivalries on the volleyball court, cricket on the rough ground, and sitting on the sidelines gossiping.

06:30 PM

Evening Prep

Two hours of absolute silence (supposedly). Passing notes, finishing homework, and hiding novels inside textbooks.

10:00 PM

Lights Out

House master rounds, whispering in the dark, ghost stories, and planning the next day. The true bonding hours.
